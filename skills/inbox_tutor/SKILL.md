# SKILL: inbox_tutor

## Workflow
1. **Summarize**: Read the new article in the inbox. Present a concise summary of the core thesis to the user.
2. **Challenge**: Ask a Socratic question to test their understanding and how it challenges their current thinking.
3. **Verify Mastery**: Wait for the user's response and engage in a dialogue. Continue until the user demonstrates a sufficient understanding of the core concepts.
